ClubHub supports four roles: Student, Club Executive, ICBT Admin, and Site Admin. Students may register freely. Executive accounts are created only by the ICBT Admin team. Clubs and societies on ClubHub are created through an approval process managed by the ICBT Admin team. Club products are listed by club executives and approved by ICBT Admin. Orders placed through ClubHub are managed by the respective club. Stock quantities are automatically adjusted when an order is placed. Any issues regarding product delivery, quality, or refunds should be directed to the issuing club.
