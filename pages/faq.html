Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using ClubHub.

01 Getting Started

How do I create a ClubHub account?

Click Register on the home page or login modal. Fill in your Student ID, full name, email, password, phone number, and semester. Agree to the Terms & Conditions and submit. You can then log in with your email and password.

Is ClubHub free to use?

Yes. ClubHub is provided free of charge to all enrolled ICBT students and staff.

Can I browse without logging in?

Yes. Guests can browse the public event feed and the list of clubs and societies. To join a club, RSVP to events, place orders, add reminders, or request a new club, you must be logged in.

02 Clubs & Societies

How do I join a club?

Go to Clubs & Societies, open the club you want to join, and click Request to join. The club's executive board reviews every request. Once approved, you will see the club under My Clubs & Societies.

Can I be a member of more than one club?

Yes. You can join as many clubs and societies as you like. Each club appears as a tab inside My Clubs & Societies.

What does the club member view give me?

Once you are an approved member, you can open the club dashboard and see events, meetings, board members, projects, products, announcements, the gallery, and full team chat. The member view is read-only — only executives can add or edit club content.

How do I start a new club?

From the sidebar, open Request a Club. Fill in the proposed name, category, description, purpose, expected members, and optionally attach a cover letter. The ICBT Admin team reviews the request. If approved, the club is created and you are contacted about setting up an executive account.

How do I leave a club?

Contact the club's executive board or the ICBT Admin team. They can remove you from the club's membership list. Your past orders and activity remain on record.

03 Events & RSVPs

Why isn't my event showing on the public feed?

New events created by club executives go to the ICBT Admin team for approval first. Once approved they appear in the public feed. The public feed only shows upcoming events — past events stay visible on the club's own page.

What happens if an event is full?

If an event has a capacity and it is already full, you can still join the waitlist. If someone cancels, the next person on the waitlist is promoted automatically and receives a notification.

How do reminders work?

Click the bookmark icon on any event to set a reminder. The system records a reminder time 24 hours before the event and an expiry 24 hours after. All reminders are available under Reminders in the sidebar.

Can I cancel my RSVP?

Yes. Click the heart icon again (or use Cancel RSVP on the event detail page). If someone was on the waitlist, they will be promoted.

04 Orders & Products

Who can sell products on ClubHub?

Only club executives can list products, and each product must be approved by the ICBT Admin team before it appears on the club page.

How do I place an order?

Browse a club's products, click Order, enter the quantity and your contact details. You must be an approved member of the club to order. Orders can be tracked under My Orders.

Is stock updated automatically?

Yes. When an order is placed, the available stock is reduced immediately. If there is not enough stock, the order is declined.

05 Account & Profile

How do I change my profile details?

Open the profile menu (top-right) and click My Profile. You can update your full name, phone number, semester, and profile photo. Student ID, email, and role are locked and can only be changed by an administrator.

What if I forget my password?

Click Forgot password? on the login modal. You will be shown the contact details to visit ICBT Student Affairs with your student ID. An administrator will verify your identity and issue a new password.

What are notifications?

The bell icon in the top-right shows updates relevant to you — new events, join request approvals or rejections, club request decisions, and new team chat messages. Unread items show a red dot on the bell and next to the relevant sidebar menu.

06 Support

How do I report a bug or issue?

Click the Report button at the bottom-right of any page. Describe the issue and submit. Reports are reviewed by the Site Admin team.

How do I contact support?

Use the Help link in the top navigation bar, or email the ICBT Student Affairs office at studentaffairs@icbt.lk.

Still Have Questions?

Reach out to the ICBT Student Affairs office or submit a report through the platform. We are happy to help.
